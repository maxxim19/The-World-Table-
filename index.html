Our story

“We learned to host at tables where every guest came from somewhere else.”

Our founders grew up around residences and receptions where people from dozens of countries shared a meal. One was raised in a diplomatic household, where hosting the world was part of ordinary life.

Those evenings taught us that a thoughtful menu is a way of welcoming someone. The World Table brings that same attention to every dinner, celebration and reception we cater.
